MECHANISM. The tax is not noise: it reproduces stage for stage across
independent reps, and it is largest on the largest, most register-hungry
stages (W13, then attention and W2) while the small elementwise stages
barely move. That is the shape of a SHARED-REGISTER-BUDGET effect, and gate
2 measured the cause -- persistent_kernel went 238 -> 255 registers (the
ceiling at __launch_bounds__(256,1)) and picked up a 4-byte spill that the
base tree does not have. The v013 candidate holds 16 live u64 sorted keys
plus the buffered top-k across its k-loop, and that is what consumed the
headroom every other task body was sharing.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CAMPAIGN, not just for this issue: the tree now starts
at the register ceiling with a spill. The next register-hungry integration
inherits that, and its own ptxas before/after has to be read against this
baseline rather than against the pre-M4-I6 one.
