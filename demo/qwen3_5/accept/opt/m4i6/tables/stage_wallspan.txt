READING IT:

  The profiler inflates per-task time, so arm A's T will not equal M4-I5's
  recorded T exactly; the 'profiler basis ratio' column is that offset,
  stated rather than hidden. The RECOVERY is computed from the A->B pair
  measured in the same session, which is the comparison that is sound; the
  rescaled path contribution is the same delta expressed in M4-I5's units
  and is a projection, not a measurement.

  A stage at concurrency ~1.0 has NO width residual to hide behind: what
  the kernel saves, the step collects, minus only whatever the step's
  other stages were already overlapping. That is why the honest test of
  this integration is the e2e A/B, and why the e2e delta should land close
  to the wallspan delta rather than far below it.
