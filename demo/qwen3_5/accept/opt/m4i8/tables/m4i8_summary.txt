4. FULL FAN-IN RELAXATION IS REFUTED BY THE TRACE, NOT BY ARGUMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Every event is a full fan-in barrier (num_triggers == n_producers, 2277/2277).
   But of the ~500 ms of worker idle per step, the amount recoverable by looking
   past a blocked queue head is:
     bs1   idle  474466 us  poll  30758  starved  438572  |  fully recoverable (W<=64) 0  partially (W=8) 1837
     bs8   idle  499067 us  poll  37535  starved  456332  |  fully recoverable (W<=64) 0  partially (W=8) 2348
     bs16  idle  522635 us  poll  49998  starved  466984  |  fully recoverable (W<=64) 0  partially (W=8) 2480
   hol_full is ZERO at every window: tasks are prelaunched in graph position
   order, that order is topological, so each worker's queue is itself
   topologically ordered and its head is always its most-ready task. In-order
   draining is already optimal GIVEN this assignment.
