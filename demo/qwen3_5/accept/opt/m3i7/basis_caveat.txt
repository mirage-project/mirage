Two caveats, both structural rather than fixable by re-running. (1) bs16 still has no full-width prefill-free window at ANY context -- staggered admission means the 16 requests never all decode simultaneously, so its step is measured over the widest available regime (12 of 16 live, 13 iterations, per-slot contexts spread across ~263-890). Read bs16 as directionally right, not as precise as bs1/bs8. bs1 (1 live, the whole 640-step decode) and bs8 (8 live, 486 iterations) are clean. (2) The MPK column is one step from one profiled rep per bs; the wave wall it sits inside was measured over three unprofiled reps with a full range of 0.02-3.3% (opt/m3i7/tables/), and per-step task-event counts are schedule-determined, so a single profiled rep is sufficient for the decomposition but the ABSOLUTE step is still a single-rep number. Stage ratios, which are what the targets are ranked on, are ratios inside one capture and are insensitive to that.
