(test_kda_moe_layer.py --order moe; rank kinds moeaggkda / moeaggmla; MPK_K3_MOEAGGKDA / _MOEAGGMLA). moe_agg1 posts its

## 4. Where the remaining gap is (measured)

- **Streaming is at the hardware limit:** a pure 1 KB-per-wave global->LDS copy of 384 KB per CU takes ~19 us
  (5.1-5.35 TB/s including ~4 us ramp; ~6.4 TB/s once streaming). The KDA projection (92 MB, 384 KB on its busiest
  CUs) ends at 20.4-22.7 us: at that limit.
- **Cross-GPU hop:** ~4.1 us per xGMI hop regardless of size (B300 NVLink ~1-2 us); ~3 hops per layer.
- **Kernel boundaries:** no PDL on AMD; each kernel pays ~4-5 us launch + ramp; per layer: agg1 (SGLang), attention,
  MoE = 3 launches.
- **MoE expert phase:** ~197 MB in ~50 us; single items already stream at the per-CU limit; the losses are the W13
  half items, the start ramp and the W13 -> W2 transition.
- **Attention residual:** its own work is ~2 us (knockout build); its tail is the hop and the final stores.
